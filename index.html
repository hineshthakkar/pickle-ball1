The best kind of contagious

Easy to learn.
Hard to leave.

Pickleball is a paddle sport that borrows the best bits from tennis, badminton, and table tennis. It's played on a small court with a perforated ball—and has a knack for turning “just one game” into an afternoon.

New to the game? Perfect. You can learn the basics in minutes, and the community is always happy to show you the ropes.

Three steps to your first rally

That's all it takes
to get in the game.

No fancy kit or years of practice required. Just bring a little curiosity and a willingness to have fun.

01 / Grab a paddle

Borrow or bring

Most courts have paddles to borrow. Wear comfy clothes and court-friendly shoes.

02 / Learn the basics

Serve underhand

Serve diagonally, let the ball bounce once on each side, then rally away.

03 / Find your people

Play, laugh, repeat

Join an open-play session near you. Doubles is popular, and beginners are welcome.
